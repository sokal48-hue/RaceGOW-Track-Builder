1 · Load a track

Sample cube and START gate · physical geometry stays locked

2 · FIRST: Select required gate sequence

First choose Select gates, then click gate opening diamonds on the 3D track in racing order. Every occurrence gets a distinct color, even if the same gate is repeated. Click the green Forward or orange Reverse arrow extending from the selected diamond; gates may be used repeatedly. Generate a route when finished; edit individual checkpoints below if necessary.
Direction legend: GREEN F = Forward (− axis to + axis); ORANGE R = Reverse (+ axis to − axis). Click a diamond to reveal the two arrows, then click the arrow for your desired direction. No popup. These directions stay fixed to the track even when you rotate the camera.
Each numbered gate pass gets a different color. The route changes color just after that gate is crossed. Repeat passes get new colors.
Use the active elevation to display gate diamonds clearly; toggle All gates when needed. Only the middle of START/FINISH is the lap boundary; nearby openings are checkpoints. Click the direction button beside a selected gate to reverse its crossing.

3 · SECOND: Draw and edit path

Select the gate sequence above FIRST. Then in Draw flight path mode, hold left-click and move to sketch the flight route. While holding, press ↑ or ↓ to climb or descend to another elevation. Release to save the stroke as editable points. Choose a colored elevation level, then click anywhere on its plane to add a checkpoint. Only checkpoints at the selected level can be moved or removed. Other levels stay visible but locked. Generated routes include diamond-shaped A apex handles inside every route segment. Choose the apex elevation, switch to Select / move, and drag an A to shape the turn. Gate checkpoints remain where they were. Select an A apex and choose Convert apex to checkpoint to make it a regular numbered checkpoint at exactly the same position.
Each elevation has its own checkpoint, path and plane color. Select a level to edit its checkpoints; Raise/Lower switches the active level.
The last checkpoint is anchored to START/FINISH and cannot be moved or deleted. Generate or add points before it.

4 · Preview the lap
