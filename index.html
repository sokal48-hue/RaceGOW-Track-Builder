Track Designer

Build and share the physical course in an interactive 6×6×6 workspace.

  • Gate, cube, and START/FINISH presets
  • Automatic connector and inventory calculations
  • Editable or locked JSON track files
  • Step-by-step printable build guides

Flight Path Designer

Plan gate sequences and create a racing line through your track.

  • Required gate sequence and crossing directions
  • Mouse-drawn flight paths with elevation controls
  • Editable checkpoints and turn apexes
  • Smoothed lap preview and route colors
